How it all fits together

One idea runs through the chapter: what we eat becomes what we are — every nutrient has a job, missing one causes disease, and how far our food travels matters too.

How the ideas connect

Links worth saying aloud — this is what turns facts into understanding.

1 · Jobs explain the diseases

Each vitamin or mineral has a job; when it is missing, that job fails — Vitamin A for eyes → night blindness, iron for blood → anaemia.

2 · Tests prove what's inside

The iodine, paper and copper-sulphate tests let you check, not guess, which nutrients a food has — the scientific method from Chapter 1 in action.

3 · Balanced diet ties it all together

No single food (not even millets) has everything — a balanced diet combines energy-giving, body-building and protective foods with roughage and water.

4 · Health and planet

Local, plant-based food is healthier and has fewer food miles — good for our bodies and our environment.
