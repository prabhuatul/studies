The Wonderful World of Science

Read top to bottom — it follows the order of the chapter. Tick each section off as you finish it.

1 · What science is

  • Science = "a way of thinking, observing and doing things to understand the world we live in and to uncover the secrets of the universe."
  • The most important thing: Curiosity (the title of the textbook).
  • Science is everywhere: ocean depths ↔ outer space, kitchen ↔ playground. Big discoveries often come from unexpected places.

2 · The jigsaw puzzle idea

PuzzleScience
Each new pieceEach new discovery
Puzzle never endsNo limit — every answer leads to more questions
A piece in the wrong place is movedNew discoveries change our understanding
Pieces fit togetherIdeas are all connected

3 · The scientific method — learn the order

Most-asked part of the chapterSay the five steps in order without looking.
#StepPen example
1ObserveThe pen has stopped writing.
2Question"Why did my pen stop writing?"
3GuessThe ink is finished.
4TestOpen the pen and check the refill.
5AnalyseEmpty → guess correct. Not empty → new guess (ink dried up) and test again.
Watch outA guess is tested by experiments or more observations — not by asking someone or by memorising.

4 · Everyday scientists

Anyone who follows the scientific method is working like a scientist — "in a way, we are all scientists!"

5 · What the book will explore

  • Planet Earth — the only planet known to support life · variety of plants and animals
  • Food and what it is made of · materials and how to separate them
  • Water (rain, ice, steam) · hot and cold (how hot is it?)
  • Beyond Earth — the Sun, Moon and stars

6 · 10 facts most likely to be asked

  1. Science is a way of thinking, observing and doing things.
  2. The most important thing for science is curiosity.
  3. Science is like a giant, unending jigsaw puzzle.
  4. Steps: Observe, Question, Guess, Test, Analyse.
  5. A guess is tested through experiments or more observations.
  6. Wrong guess → make a new guess and test again.
  7. Earth is the only planet we know that supports life.
  8. Anyone using the scientific method is working like a scientist.
  9. Science is rarely done alone — scientists work in large teams.
  10. "To be a wise person, you must be a whys person!"

Common mistakes to avoid

Don't lose marks here
  • Saying science is "just memorising facts" or "just doing experiments" — the chapter says it is a step-by-step process for finding answers.
  • Getting the steps out of order — you guess before you test, and you analyse after testing.
  • Thinking only people in laboratories are scientists — anyone using the method is working like one.
  • Thinking a wrong guess means the method failed — it simply leads to a new guess.
  • Thinking scientific knowledge never changes — new discoveries can move a misplaced piece of the puzzle.
