ai — as in eye (ಐ)
ru — ಋ (as in ಕೃ = kru)
t, d — soft, tongue touching the teeth (ತ, ದ)
T, D, N, L — capital = curl the tongue back (ಟ, ಡ, ಣ, ಳ)
th, dh, kh, gh, bh — with a puff of air (ಥ, ಧ, ಖ, ಘ, ಭ)
sh — ಶ / ಷ   ·  ch — ಚ

A doubled consonant (tt, ll, TT) is held a little longer: ಮತ್ತು = mat-tu. Hyphens just split long words into easy pieces.
