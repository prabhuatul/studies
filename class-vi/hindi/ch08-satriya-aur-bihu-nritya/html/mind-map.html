How the ideas connect

खेती → बिहूMost people in Assam are farmers, so their biggest festival follows the farming year: sowing, planting, harvest. That is why there are three Bihus.
बसंत → नया साल → बोहाग बिहूApril brings spring and the Assamese New Year. That is why Angela, arriving in April, saw Bihu on her very first night.
मठ → सत्रिया → बदलावSattriya was born in the Satras and danced by monks. When monks began teaching outsiders, women could learn it too. That is why women now perform it on stage.
नृत्य → भावनाएँ → दोस्तीDance expresses feelings in every culture. That is how a London girl and an Assamese girl became friends and shared the same dances.
