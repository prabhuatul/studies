How it all fits together

How the ideas connect

Five links worth saying aloud — this is what turns facts into understanding.

1 · One test for everything

Every example in the chapter — pilots, carpenters, grandparents, langars — answers the same question: did money change hands? That single test sorts the whole chapter.

2 · Pay has four shapes

Salary, fee, wage, payment in kind aren't random vocabulary — each fits a specific kind of work: regular employment, professional advice, time-based labour, or non-cash exchange.

3 · Value addition is visible with numbers

Rajesh's chair turns an abstract idea — "work creates value" — into simple arithmetic: ₹1,000 − ₹600 = ₹400 of pure human skill and effort.

4 · Non-economic doesn't mean worthless

The chapter goes out of its way to say non-economic activities matter — sevā, family care, and volunteering all create real value, just not a price tag.

5 · Individual acts become collective strength

Swachh Bharat Abhiyan and Van Mahotsav show non-economic effort scaling up: one person's small act of care becomes a nationwide movement when many people do it together.
