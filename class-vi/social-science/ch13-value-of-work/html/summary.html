3 · Value addition — Rajesh's chair

Wood costs ₹600 → chair sells for ₹1,000 → ₹400 value added (Rajesh's skill, time, effort)

Value addition = the extra monetary value created at each stage of turning something into another form.

5 · Sevā and non-economic value

  • Sevā = selfless service — temples, gurudwaras, mosques, churches.
  • Langar = community kitchen (e.g. Golden Temple) serving free food to every visitor.
  • Fosters satisfaction and gratitude; contributes to society without expecting anything in return.

7 · Ten facts most likely to be asked

  1. Economic = involves money/money's worth; non-economic = no payment
  2. Market = place of exchange of goods/services
  3. Fee = paid for professional services (doctor, lawyer)
  4. Salary = fixed, regular, usually monthly
  5. Wage = pay for a specific period of work
  6. Payment in kind = non-cash payment
  7. Rajesh's chair: ₹600 wood → ₹1,000 sale → ₹400 value added
  8. Sevā = selfless service; langar = free community kitchen
  9. Swachh Bharat Abhiyan = cleanliness; Van Mahotsav = tree planting
  10. Non-economic activities boost social welfare, wellbeing, quality of life

Common mistakes to avoid

Don't lose marks here
  • Saying Geeta Aunty's piloting is "non-economic" because she "serves the country" — she is paid a salary, so it's economic.
  • Mixing up salary (regular, monthly) with wage (for a specific period) — they are not the same thing.
  • Forgetting that payment in kind still counts as pay, even though it's not cash.
  • Thinking non-economic activities have "no value" — the chapter explicitly says they matter for social welfare and wellbeing.
  • Getting the value-addition subtraction backwards — it's selling price minus cost, not the other way round.
  • Forgetting that one person can do both an economic job and a non-economic (volunteer) activity.
