## Deck 6 · Time, Time Zones & the Date Line (cards 36–40)

| # | Front | Back |
|---|---|---|
| 36 | How many degrees does the Earth turn in one hour, and why? | **15°** — because one full turn of **360°** takes **24 hours**, and 360 ÷ 24 = 15. |
| 37 | Going east from the Prime Meridian, does time move ahead or behind? | **Ahead** — add an hour for every 15°. Going **west**, subtract. |
| 38 | What is IST, and how far ahead of GMT is it? | **Indian Standard Time** — **5 hours 30 minutes** ahead of **GMT** (Greenwich Mean Time). |
| 39 | Why aren't time-zone boundaries straight lines? | They bend to respect each country's standard time, so they tend to **follow international borders**. |
| 40 | What is the International Date Line, and what happens when you cross it? | The line at approximately **180°** longitude, opposite the Prime Meridian. Crossing it **eastward you subtract a day**; **westward you add a day**. |
