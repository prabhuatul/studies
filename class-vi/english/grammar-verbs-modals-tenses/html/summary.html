2 · Signal words

  • every day, always, usually → simple present
  • now, at the moment, Look! → present continuous
  • already, just, yet, since, for → present perfect
  • yesterday, last…, …ago → simple past
  • while, at 6 pm yesterday → past continuous
  • before, by the time (past) → past perfect
  • tomorrow, next…, soon → simple future

7 · 10 facts most likely to be asked

  1. Continuous = be + V-ing.
  2. Perfect = have/has/had + V3.
  3. Modal + V1 — no -s, -ing or -ed.
  4. He/She/It → is, was, has, does, V1+s.
  5. Past perfect = the earlier of two past actions.
  6. Present continuous for "now".
  7. Simple present for habits ("every Sunday").
  8. must = compulsion; should = advice; can = ability.
  9. used to = past habit; need to = necessity.
  10. many + countable; much + uncountable.

Common mistakes to avoid

Don't lose marks here
  • "Did he went?" ✗ → "Did he go?" ✓ — after did, always V1.
  • "She can sings" ✗ → "She can sing" ✓ — after a modal, always V1.
  • "I have finished it yesterday" ✗ → "I finished it yesterday" ✓.
  • "The children is playing" ✗ → "The children are playing" ✓ — children is plural.
  • Using V2 in perfect tenses: "has wrote" ✗ → "has written" ✓.
  • "There are much flowers" ✗ → "many flowers" ✓.
  • Changing the subject or object when converting tense — only the verb group changes.
